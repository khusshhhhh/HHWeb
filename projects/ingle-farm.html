Ingle Farm Homes.

One corner block. Two new homes. Built and handed over.

LocationIngle Farm, SA 5093
Type2 single-storey homes
StatusConstruction complete

01 / Overview

Four bedrooms, all on one level.

Two single-storey homes on Lots 1 and 2, 44 Albara Road.

The site sits on the corner of Albara Road and Eudunda Avenue in Ingle Farm, in Adelaide's north-east. It was split into two lots, with a new four-bedroom home built on each. Both are now complete.

Both homes have 2.7 m ceilings, with a raised ceiling over the family and meals area. Lot 1 adds a study. Lot 2 adds a separate living room and a double garage.
