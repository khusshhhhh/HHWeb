01 / Overview

Five bedrooms, a theatre and a pool.

67 Coorara Avenue, Payneham South.

A new home designed around one family's brief. Downstairs, a 58 m² open living area runs to the alfresco and pool, with a kitchen and butler's pantry, home theatre, lounge, study and prayer room. A guest bedroom with its own ensuite sits on the ground floor.

Upstairs, the master bedroom has a walk-in robe and ensuite, with three more bedrooms, two bathrooms, a rumpus room and a balcony to the street. The design has development approval and building rules consent, and the home is now under construction.
