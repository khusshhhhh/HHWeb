01 / Overview

Living below, sleeping above.

Four two-storey homes on one 784 m² site.

Davoren Park sits in Adelaide's northern suburbs. The project places four townhouses on a single site, each with living areas below and bedrooms above.

Each home puts living areas on the ground floor and bedrooms upstairs. Hills & Harbour managed the project from site assessment through to full development approval.
