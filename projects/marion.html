01 / Overview

Two homes, side by side.

One Marion site, split into two lots.

The site is split into two lots of 293.16 m² each, with a single-storey home on each. Both homes have four bedrooms, with an ensuite and walk-in robe to the main bedroom.

Each home has an open kitchen and meals area, a family bathroom, a garage to the street and an alfresco at the rear. The City of Marion has granted development approval.
