Glenunga Residences.

Four two-storey homes on Portrush Road. Planning drawings issued for approval.

LocationGlenunga, SA 5064
Type4 two-storey homes
StatusIn planning

01 / Overview

Four homes along one shared drive.

593 Portrush Road, split into four lots.

The site at 593 Portrush Road, Glenunga, is 30.48 m wide and 72.24 m deep. The plan places four two-storey homes in a row, each on its own lot, served by one shared driveway with visitor parking.

Each home has a guest bedroom with ensuite downstairs, and two main bedrooms upstairs, each with its own ensuite and walk-in robe. Downstairs there is a lounge, open living and dining, kitchen and laundry. Every home has a double garage and an alfresco, and three of the four have a balcony.
