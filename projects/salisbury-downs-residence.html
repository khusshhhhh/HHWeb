01 / Overview

One home on a corner block.

Four bedrooms, a double garage to the side street.

A single-storey home on a 364.81 m² corner block. The main bedroom has an ensuite and walk-in robe, with three more bedrooms and a family bathroom.

The kitchen opens to the family and dining area and out to an alfresco. The double garage faces the side street, leaving the main frontage to the entry and garden.
