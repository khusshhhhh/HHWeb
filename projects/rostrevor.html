01 / Overview

A four-bedroom home in the foothills.

4 Moyle Avenue, Rostrevor.

A single-storey home with four bedrooms. The master suite has an ensuite and walk-in robe, and the three other bedrooms each have a built-in robe and share a family bathroom.

A front lounge sits off the entry. At the rear, the kitchen with walk-in pantry opens to the dining and family area and out to an alfresco. There is also a powder room, laundry, linen cupboard and a single garage.
