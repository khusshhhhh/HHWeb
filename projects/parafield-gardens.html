01 / Overview

Two homes, side by side.

18 Taylor Street, Parafield Gardens.

One Taylor Street site becomes two lots, each with a new single-storey home and its own driveway. Both homes have three bedrooms, with an ensuite to the main bedroom, a double garage and an alfresco.

The City of Salisbury has granted planning consent under the Planning, Development and Infrastructure Act 2016.
