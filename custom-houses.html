Service 01

One home. Planned around your block.

For owners building a single new home. We assess the land, coordinate design and approvals, and manage the build through to handover.

Talk to us →
Indicative imagery

The service

You make the decisions. We make them happen.

A custom home involves a dozen parties: agent, surveyor, designer, engineer, certifier, council, builder, bank. Each one waits on another. We sit in the middle and keep them all moving.

You get one point of contact, one schedule and one set of numbers, from the first look at a block to the day you get the keys.

See the six steps →

Who it's for

Three ways to start.

A

You already own a block.

We check what it will take to build there, then take the project from design brief to handover.

B

You're still looking.

We help find and assess land before you buy, so the block suits the home you want and the budget you have.

C

You're knocking down to rebuild.

Demolition, services and the new build, coordinated so the gap between old house and new is as short as possible.

Six steps to the keys.

Each step ends with a clear decision for you and a written record of where things stand.

What's included

What you get from us.

The same four things on every project, whatever its size.

01

One schedule.

Every task, consultant and consent on one timeline, updated as things move.

02

Weekly reports.

Photos, progress and next steps in plain language, every week of the build.

Cost tracking.

Independent eyes.

We inspect on your behalf at each stage, not the builder's.
