Our story

We keep projects moving.

Most new homes are delayed in the gaps: between the agent and the surveyor, the designer and the council, the builder and the bank. We set up Hills & Harbour to close those gaps.

We work across the whole journey: finding and assessing land, subdividing it, managing approvals and coordinating the build of new homes. One team, one schedule, one set of numbers.

The name says where we work. The hills and the harbour frame Adelaide, and every project we take on sits somewhere between them.

How we work

Four principles.

They shape every report, schedule and conversation.

01

Facts, shown plainly.

Real figures, real dates, named sources. A number beats an adjective.

02

One schedule.

Land, design, approvals and build sit on one timeline that you can see at any time.

03

No gaps.

We coordinate every consultant, so nothing waits on a handover between them.

04

Built to hand over.

Every decision is made with the day you get the keys in mind.

Process

Six stages of build.

Always in this order. We run each stage and the joins between them.

01

Land

Site search, appraisal, zoning checks and the true cost to build.

02

Slab

Set-out, footings and the slab, inspected before framing starts.

03

Framing

Wall frames, floor and roof trusses, checked against the plans.

04

Internal Works

Services, insulation, plaster, cabinetry and finishes.

05

External Works

Cladding, roofing, windows, driveway, fencing and landscaping.

06

Handover

Defects fixed, certificates collected, keys handed over.
